06 / FAQ

Good questions.
We have answers.

Do you provide free estimates?+

Yes. Every project starts with a complimentary estimate based on your property, desired coverage, and lighting system.

Do I need to know my roofline measurements?+

No. Approximate measurements are helpful, but if you aren't sure, send photos and the information you do have. We'll help determine what's needed.

Do you install permanent lighting year-round?+

Yes. The system is designed to stay installed year-round, so you can use it for everyday accent lighting, holidays, events and more.

When is the deposit due?+

After you approve your estimate and lighting design, a 50% deposit is required to secure the project and order materials. The remaining balance is due upon completion.

Do you only install Christmas lighting?+

No. Permanent lighting can be used for everyday warm white lighting, holidays, sports, parties and custom colors throughout the year.
